About B.U.D.D.H.I.

Binary Understanding Data Driven Humanoid Intelligence

B.U.D.D.H.I. is an offline assistant. It runs on your own device, so answering a question needs no cloud servers or data-centre cooling.

It finds real passages in its local collection and shows them with their sources. It is not a language model: it does not write new text, and it tells you when it has nothing relevant instead of guessing.

Where the knowledge comes from

The desktop app has every passage; the version inside Claude leaves out some biographies to fit Claude's 256 MB page limit.
